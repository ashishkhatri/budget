Import statements

Files are read by the app on this computer and saved to your local database.

Best: CSV

In Chase, open an account → Download account activity (the down-arrow icon) → choose CSV and a date range. CSV is exact; PDF reading is a best guess, so you'll get a preview to check either way. Overlapping date ranges are fine: duplicates are skipped.

Bank connections

Download transactions straight from your bank through Plaid instead of importing files. You sign in to your bank in Plaid's window (for Chase, on chase.com), so your password never reaches this app. The connection is read-only: it can see transactions, not move money.

Local AI

Suggests categories for anything the normal rules leave Uncategorized, using a model running in LM Studio on this computer. Nothing leaves your machine. Changes save automatically.
